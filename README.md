# Divine Enterprise Operations & Financial Intelligence Platform

A 24/7 autonomous full-stack enterprise resource suite for sales, inventory tracking, courier RTO, customer return physical QC grading, marketing attribution, and bank ledger reconciliation.

## Features
- **Real-Time Stock Matrix**: Tracks usable stock on hand, quarantine holding pools, and 4-tier stock health badges.
- **Reverse Logistics Engine**: 3-stage RTO pipeline and Customer Return Physical QC grading (Grade A, B, Damaged, Dispute).
- **Zero-Drift Financial Accounting**: Dispatched Gross Revenue vs. Adjusted Realized Cash after returns, reverse courier fees, and damaged stock write-offs.
- **Fast Record Sale Modal**: Global quick transaction intake.
- **Excel & CSV Dual-Sync**: Automatic synchronization with master Excel workbooks and 3NF flat CSV ledgers.
